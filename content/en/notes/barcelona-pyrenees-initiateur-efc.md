After finishing secondary school exams I needed a change of scene. The EFC (École Française de Descente de Canyon) was running an **Initiateur** (assistant instructor) course in the Pyrenees in late June — too good to miss. Departed 18 June, five days after exams ended, via Barcelona.

**Barcelona** (18–20 and 28–30 June): impressive urban grid, wide boulevards, cycle lanes, parks.

**EFC Initiateur course, Pyrenees**: technical canyoning instruction and beginner teaching methodology, practised in southeast French canyons.
