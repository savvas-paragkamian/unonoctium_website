The 4th Pan-Cretan Speleology Symposium took place in Zoniana on 25–26 October 2008, attended by 65 participants from Crete, Athens and Thessaloniki. Twenty-two talks covered new cave discoveries, environmental education, geology, palaeontology, archaeology, biology and cave management.

My first conference presentation: speleological research in Sitanos (Siteia, Lasithi), co-authored with Kostis Foteinakis, Afroditi Kardamaki and Georgia Papadokostaki.
