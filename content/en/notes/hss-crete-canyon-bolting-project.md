The HSS Crete Section has included the re-rigging of ten well-known Cretan canyons in its action plan. Goal: install additional plates so every waterfall meets the **two-point anchor rule**, and build horizontal traverses on exposed rappels. On completion the canyons will be suitable for both guided descents and training.
