Kamaraiko is the largest technical canyon in Crete: 5km long with 1260m altitude difference between entrance and exit, and one of the most beautiful. Located near the village of Kamares on Mount Psiloritis.

Visited on 01.07.2007 as part of POEF explorations, with Yiannis Bromirakis, Katerina Hatzidimitriou, Stavros Zembilis, Eleni Diarivianaki.
