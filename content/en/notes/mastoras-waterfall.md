03.02.2008 — the Mastoras waterfall was rigged for descent. This lateral gorge pours vertically into Ha canyon at **215 metres** — the **tallest waterfall in Crete**.

Team: Yiannis Bromirakis, Katerina Hatzidimitriou, Maya Hristodoulaki, Savas Paragkamian.

Effect on Ha canyon: the final third of Ha is typically dry before this point; after the Mastoras inflow it runs with heavy water.
