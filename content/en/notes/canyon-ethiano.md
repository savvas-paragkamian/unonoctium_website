13 September 2009 with Yiannis Vargiyakakis, Afroditi Kardamaki, Manos Petrakis and Stelios Lamprinos. Ethiano canyon lies in the Asterousia range: entrance at Ethia village (670m), exit at Rotasi (282m). Carved through the Ethia series platy limestone — the **spectacular fold structures** in the walls are the canyon's defining feature.

First descended by Yiannis Bromirakis and Yiannis Skontinakis in 2005. Twelve technical descents, longest 37m (the middle section of a broken 65m waterfall). We used a tyrolean traverse to avoid a 1.4m-deep brown pool — which deepens when you're carrying a pack.

This time, no cameras were destroyed.
