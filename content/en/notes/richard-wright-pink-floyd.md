Richard Wright died on 15.09.2008 aged 65 from cancer. Founding keyboardist of Pink Floyd (1965), alongside Syd Barrett, Roger Waters and Nick Mason. He left after *The Wall* due to disagreements with Waters, returning in 1986.

Albums: *Dark Side Of The Moon* (1973), *Wish You Were Here* (1975), *Animals* (1977), *The Wall* (1979), *The Final Cut* (1983), *The Division Bell* (1994).
