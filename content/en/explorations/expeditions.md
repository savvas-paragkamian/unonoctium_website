Multi-day field campaigns into remote and extreme terrain — deep-cave expeditions, island sampling days, coastal traverses. Logistics for teams of thirty or more, where simply reaching the sampling site is the hardest part of the science. From the Psari massif of the White Mountains to the TREC traverse of European coastlines.
