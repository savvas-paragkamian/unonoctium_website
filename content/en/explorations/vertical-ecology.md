Life in the vertical dimension — raptor birds nesting on sheer rock faces, studied and sampled by rope access across the Aegean and Crete. Bonelli's eagle, golden eagle and the bearded vulture (*Gypaetus barbatus*) live where few researchers can reach; technical climbing becomes a method for ecology.
